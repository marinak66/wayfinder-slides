## Design Philosophy

Slides should feel like a **polished conference talk**, not a wall of text.
Every slide must earn its place, if it's just bullet points that could be an email, rethink it.

**Fewer slides, more depth.** Target ~2 minutes per content slide. A 45-minute session should have roughly 18-22 content slides (including section dividers and quizzes), not 30+. Each slide should have enough substance that the presenter spends real time on it. If a slide only takes 30 seconds to present, it doesn't deserve to exist or merge it into another slide.

Key principles:
- **Never invent processes, lifecycles, or terminology** - use ONLY the terms, step names, phase names, and concepts that appear in the CoE source material. If the source describes steps as "Requirements, Dimensions, Technology Landscape", use those exact words - do NOT rename them to "Discover, Extract, Map, Validate" or any other made-up labels. The source is the single source of truth for all terminology.
- **Show, don't tell** - whenever you explain a UI concept, pair it with a real screenshot.
- **Use images generously** - the prompt includes an image catalog with CoE screenshots. Use them.
- **Quizzes -> dark theme** - quiz slides automatically get the dark layout; keep questions clear with 4 options.
- **Speaker notes are essential** - every slide needs rich speaker notes (3-5 sentences) that a presenter can read verbatim.
