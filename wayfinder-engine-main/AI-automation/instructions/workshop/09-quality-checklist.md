## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:
- [ ] No two consecutive `standard` slides - break them up with a visual layout.
- [ ] Every UI concept has an accompanying screenshot (if available in the catalog).
- [ ] Speaker notes on every slide (3-5 sentences, can be read verbatim).
- [ ] Quiz questions have clear wrong answers and one unambiguous correct answer (training format only - workshops must NOT have quizzes).
- [ ] Bullet points are concise (under 15 words each).
- [ ] Total slide count is appropriate for the session duration (~2 minutes per content slide).
- [ ] Key terms are bolded with `**term**` - at least one bold word per content bullet.
- [ ] Emojis appear ONLY in two_column/three_column bodies, and only for comparison/contrast slides.
- [ ] All feedback from "Additional Notes for AI" has been addressed.
